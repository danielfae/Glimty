FAQ

Is this still on Facebook Messenger?

No. Glimty now lives on this site. The assistant is ours — it does not send you to Messenger, Typeform, or a third-party inbox.

Can I type instead of tapping buttons?

Yes. Buttons are shortcuts. A full sentence is usually enough for the assistant to skip ahead.

Do you wrap and send gifts?

The assistant takes the card line and where it should go. In this preview, checkout is simulated so you can walk the whole path.

What about the gift planner?

Add people and dates as you think of them. The list stays with your session so the next gift is already named.
