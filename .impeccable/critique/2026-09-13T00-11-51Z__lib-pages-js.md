## Overall Impression

The room is a shop counter. The first minute is a chatbot landing page that happens to own 36 SKUs. The single biggest opportunity is to make the sold mechanism (one sentence → this inventory → a kept brief) survive the header, the widget, and the product page.

2. A full Norwegian sentence builds a brief and returns three reasoned gifts.
3. Norwegian is the product language; English is a second voice.
